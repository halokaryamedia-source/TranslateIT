# Engineering Reports

## Purpose

This folder is the only location for active engineering cleanup and architecture reports.

Old reports under `DevelopingData/Reports` are retired. Keep only concise, current reports here.

## Current reports

- `StructureCleanupReport.md` - final summary of the root, DevelopingData, Documentation, Tooling, and EngineData cleanup.

## Rules

- Do not restore `DevelopingData/Reports`.
- Keep report names clear and current.
- Delete obsolete phase reports after their decisions are reflected in `DevelopingData/Documentation/Source`.
- Do not keep multiple conflicting reports for the same decision.
- New reports must point back to the source documentation when a decision becomes permanent.
