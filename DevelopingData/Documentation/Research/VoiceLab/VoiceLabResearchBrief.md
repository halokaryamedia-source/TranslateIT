# VoiceLab Research Brief

## Objective

Plan a future custom voice path for TranslateIT.

## Direction

- Prepare voice assets before live translation.
- Reuse local runtime assets during translation.
- Keep realtime latency measurable.
- Integrate through the Rust/Tauri app route.

## Future questions

1. What data quality is required?
2. How should mixed language output be represented?
3. How should voice assets be validated?
4. What can be cached or preloaded safely?
5. How should fallback behave?
